THREE STEPS TO THE PARTY

Make yourself
guild-ready.

  1. Download & extract

    Put the GratzGuild folder in your Forever client's Interface/AddOns folder. Check for Interface/AddOns/GratzGuild/GratzGuild.toc, without an extra nested folder.

  2. Enable GratzGuild

    Restart WoW if it was running during installation. Enable GratzGuild in the character-selection AddOns list.

  3. Open your ledger

    Type /gg or /gratzguild. Left-click the minimap murloc for the ledger, right-click for Settings, or drag it around the minimap.

A LITTLE ETIQUETTE. A LOT OF CONFETTI.

Your next ding, explained.

01 / THE GRATZ LEDGER

You level up.
They owe you one.

Each level you gain adds one debt to eligible guild characters, including offline members. Separate gratz messages received in the five minutes after your latest ding repay one debt each, stopping at zero. Extra replies count towards celebrations but cannot bank credit for later.

Known alts are exempt. Log into them with the addon enabled or add unvisited alts as Name-Realm in Settings. Ledgers are shared across your alts in the same guild on this local account. Tracking starts at your current level.

02 / PRESS GRATZ. SHARE THE JOY.

A big button.
A little good guild etiquette.

When a guildmate says ding or BIG DINGUS, a glowing Gratz! button appears near the upper centre-right of your screen. Nothing is sent until you left-click. One click sends your default or custom reply once to each chat destination where the queued dings appeared, including individual whispers.

The first ding gives you 30 seconds; each additional accepted ding adds 10 seconds. The button waits until you leave combat. Returning to combat hides it and pauses its countdown. Repeat dings from the same guildmate are ignored for 30 seconds, even across channels.

Rather skip this batch? Click the small X on the button. It dismisses all pending dings without sending or starting rewards. New eligible dings can still appear. Right-drag to move the button; its position is saved. In /gg settings, use Preview / Move for a safe test or Reset position to restore the default. Turn off Show Gratz button to disable it.

03 / MAKE IT YOUR OWN

Your words.
Exactly your words.

Open /gg settings, edit your level-up announcement and Gratz reply, then press Save messages & phrases. The default reply is Gratz!. Custom replies are sent as written: player names are never added and placeholders are not expanded in the Gratz reply. The separate level-up announcement still supports {level} and {name}.

Settings lists all built-in gratz and ding phrases. Case, punctuation and stretched letters work: GRAAATZZZ! and Diiiing 42. Add extra gratz phrases one per line and try the phrase tester. Use Reduced motion for a brief pressed highlight instead of the orc-hand slap and bounce. The supplied wood-hit sound plays when the button is hit, respecting game sound settings.

04 / A FRIENDLY REMINDER

Keep the receipts.
Review before sending.

Select names in the Ledger, open /gg announce, review the exact preview and press Send to Guild. More than ten selected names requires confirmation. Reports send one line at a time; Cancel sending stops the remainder.

Unchecking names affects reporting only. Use Edit to correct or forgive a balance. Only chat your logged-in character actually receives can count; inaccessible chat and messages while logged out cannot.

05 / KEEP A LITTLE JOY

A collection
worth a gratz.

Press Gratz early to earn cards from the replies that follow. A real button press that sends at least one reply starts a five-minute reward window when you do not already have one. During that window, 5 incoming gratzes earn a cheer card, 10 a congratulations card, and 15 the Horde finale. They can congratulate somebody else; only eligible guildmates’ replies received after your press count. Your own messages do not count.

Each milestone plays once per window, even with the ledger closed. Further button presses keep your original deadline and progress. The reward timer continues during combat. After it expires, another valid press can start a fresh run. Your own level-up also starts a fresh five-minute run and resets the counter. Helping somebody else never creates debt or extends your personal debt-repayment window.

Open /gg celebrations to see reward progress and time remaining (view the controls ↗), or to test the regular male-orc popup. Drag the purple guide’s header to move it and its lower-right corner to resize. Tests do not unlock pictures. Real popups unlock their exact variant in /gg collection: 22 regular and rare entries shared across alts. Click unlocked pictures to view silently. Disable effects in Celebrations or use Reduced motion in Settings.
